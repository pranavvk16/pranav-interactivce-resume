About the collective

GrowFromZero is a freelance engineering collective that merges architecture, design, AI systems, and cybersecurity so ambitious teams can ship modern web, mobile, and immersive experiences without hiring a full in-house department.

We embed pods led by a systems architect, staffed with product engineers, designers, and a resident security consultant. Engagements span AI copilots, experiential commerce, platform rebuilds, and reliability programs.

The core trio—Pranav V. K (architecture & AI), Saurav S (frontend & mobile), and Gautham Rajesh (security & risk)—pull in trusted specialists from our network to match project scope, ensuring every surface from UX to compliance is covered.
